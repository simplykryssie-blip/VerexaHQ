# Verexa HQ CRM — Incident Management & Response Policy

Effective: October 2026
Owner: Verexa HQ CRM
Contact: support@verexahq.com

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the response process for suspected security incidents affecting Verexa HQ CRM or information processed through the platform.

## 2. Incident categories

Incidents may include unauthorized access or attempted access, cross-workspace data exposure, credential or secret compromise, malicious uploads or code execution attempts, payment or webhook abuse, service compromise or material availability events, and loss or unauthorized disclosure of customer information.

## 3. Detection and reporting

Potential incidents may be identified through application logs, monitoring, security testing, provider notifications, user reports, or internal review.

Reports should be sent to support@verexahq.com and should include the known time, affected feature, observed behavior, and any available evidence.

## 4. Triage

The response owner determines whether the event is a security incident, affected systems and workspaces, potential confidentiality/integrity/availability impact, whether immediate containment is required, and whether a third-party provider is involved.

## 5. Containment

Containment actions may include disabling affected functionality, revoking or rotating credentials, restricting access, blocking malicious destinations or requests, suspending affected integrations, and isolating affected workloads.

Containment should preserve evidence whenever practical.

## 6. Investigation and eradication

The response team identifies root cause, affected data and accounts, attack path, persistence, and remaining exposure. Vulnerabilities are remediated through the vulnerability-management process.

## 7. Recovery

Affected services are restored after remediation and validation. Security-sensitive recovery includes regression testing and, where appropriate, production verification.

## 8. Notification

If an incident involves customer information or creates a legally reportable event, Verexa will assess applicable contractual and legal notification obligations and notify affected parties as required.

## 9. Post-incident review

Material incidents receive a documented post-incident review covering root cause, timeline, affected systems/data, containment, remediation, notification decisions, lessons learned, and preventive actions.

## 10. Evidence

Incident evidence is preserved according to applicable legal, security, and operational requirements.
